10bb and 15bb ranges are solved: a chip-EV Nash equilibrium for shove-or-fold play with a 1bb big-blind ante, computed from a full 169×169 hand-versus-hand equity table. The solve assumes at most one player calls a shove. 25bb and deeper ranges are modeled: hands are ranked by all-in equity, weighted toward suited, connected and paired hands as stacks deepen, and each spot takes the top slice of that ranking at typical tournament frequencies. Solving those depths properly requires a full preflop solver. Every hand gets one action here; real solutions sometimes mix.
