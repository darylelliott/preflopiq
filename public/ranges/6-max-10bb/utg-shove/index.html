The spot

5 players are still to act. From the middle of the table you can add more aces, pairs and broadways. At 10bb the play is shove or fold, and the blinds and ante are a big share of your stack, so shoving ranges get wider. These ranges are a solved Nash equilibrium: against them, nobody can gain by shoving or calling differently. Here the UTG shoves about 22% of hands.
