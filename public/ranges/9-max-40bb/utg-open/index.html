The spot

8 players are still to act, and one of them wakes up with a strong hand fairly often. Play only hands that hold up when someone calls or 3-bets. At 40bb a 3-bet commits a big share of your stack, so high cards and pairs gain ground on speculative suited hands. Here the UTG opens about 14% of hands.
