The spot

The SB opens about 32% of hands. You close the action and need about 27% equity to call, and for once the big blind has position after the flop. Against a range this wide, the big blind defends a lot of hands. At 40bb a 3-bet commits a big share of your stack, so high cards and pairs gain ground on speculative suited hands.
