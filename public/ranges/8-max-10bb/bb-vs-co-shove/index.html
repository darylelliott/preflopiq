The spot

The CO shoves 10bb with about 33% of hands. Calling costs 8bb to win 11.5bb, so you need about 41% equity against that range. You close the action, so you can call wider than anyone else.

Where this range comes from

This is a solved range: a chip-EV Nash equilibrium for shove-or-fold play with a 1bb big-blind ante, where no player can gain by shoving or calling differently. How the ranges are built.
