The spot

Only the big blind is left, but you will be out of position after the flop. The small blind raises its best hands, completes (limps) with a wide middle band to keep the pot small, and folds the rest. At 40bb a 3-bet commits a big share of your stack, so high cards and pairs gain ground on speculative suited hands. Here the SB raises about 32% of hands and limps about 33%.
