The spot

Heads-up, the button posts the small blind and acts first before the flop, then has position on every later street. The button raises most hands, completes with a band of weaker ones that still play well in position, and folds the rest. At 60bb implied odds are still good, a little less than at 100bb. Here the BTN raises about 68% of hands and limps about 16%.
