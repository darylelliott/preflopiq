The spot

Only the blinds are left, and you will have position for the whole hand. With the ante, 2.5bb is in the pot before you act, so you can play wide. At 40bb a 3-bet commits a big share of your stack, so high cards and pairs gain ground on speculative suited hands. Here the BTN opens about 46% of hands.
