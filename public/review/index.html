How costs are measured

Shove-or-fold spots at 10bb and 15bb, and calls against an all-in 3-bet, use the exact expected value from the same equity table that solves the charts. At the bubble and a final table the cost is the prize equity you gave up, converted to big blinds at the table’s average chip value.

Deeper spots are estimates: they scale with the size of the pot and with how far your hand was from one where your play would have been right. Treat a small estimate as a close call and a big one as a real leak.
